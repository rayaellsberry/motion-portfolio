I’m a Motion Designer passionate about crafting dynamic visuals that tell compelling stories. My background spans sports, broadcast, and digital content, where I combine design, animation, and technical problem-solving to create work that feels polished, purposeful, and impactful. I’m a Collision Award winner and Emmy nominee, with a focus on high-energy motion graphics, visual systems, and creative storytelling. I’m always looking for ways to push ideas further, explore new tools, and make every frame count. Alongside motion design, I’m expanding into VFX and procedural workflows in Houdini, building my technical skill set and exploring new ways to bring creativity and technical execution together.
